# 🍔 Nation Fast Food Cafe

> **Crave It? Get It Here! — Deliciously Different!**

**Nation Fast Food Cafe** is a modern, responsive fast-food cafe website designed to provide customers with an attractive digital experience for exploring the cafe, viewing its menu and offers, placing orders, finding the cafe location, and sharing feedback.

The website is currently being developed for the **Opening Soon** phase and is designed to seamlessly transition to **Open Now** when the cafe officially starts operations.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Nation Fast Food Cafe is a local fast-food brand based in **Kailash Nagar, Palwal, Haryana**.

The website is designed around the cafe's official branding, including its logo, promotional poster, dark navy background, orange/golden highlights, green food accents, and bold fast-food visual style.

The goal is to create a professional online presence that allows customers to:

* Explore the cafe
* Browse the food menu
* View special offers
* Place online orders
* Order through Zomato
* Find the cafe on Google Maps
* Contact the cafe
* Submit ratings and feedback
* Learn about the cafe and its team

### 🎁 Offers

Dedicated offers section for displaying:

* Combo offers

* Discounted food items

Offers are designed to be easily updated when actual promotional details are available.

### 🛒 Online Ordering

The website is designed to support an online ordering experience where customers can:

1. Browse the menu
2. Select food items
3. Add items to cart
4. Increase/decrease quantities
5. Remove items
6. Review the order
7. Enter customer information
8. Select pickup or delivery
9. Submit the order
